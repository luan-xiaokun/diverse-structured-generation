## Decisions Made

- Reachability preserves caller input order and cardinality, preventing covered catalog structures from being diagnosed as missing obligations.
- A finite cap is the only source of `search_limit`; a completed exhaustive search never claims a resource-limit outcome.
- A reachable target with no canonical replay or an unexpected completed-search invariant break maps to `internal_error`, while replay rejection or missing target credit maps to `invalid_witness`.
