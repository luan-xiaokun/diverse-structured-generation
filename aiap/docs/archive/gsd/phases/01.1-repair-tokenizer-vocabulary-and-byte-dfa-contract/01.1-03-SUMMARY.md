## Decisions Made

- The 256 MiB limit is retained independently of package version and leaves approximately 9.8× headroom over the 27,375,184-byte representative email artifact (the CSS artifact measured 10,330,540 bytes).
- Decode rejects legacy artifacts rather than attempting migration or compatibility reconstruction.
- Serialized bytes need not match independent builds; externally observable DFA behavior and counters are the round-trip contract.
